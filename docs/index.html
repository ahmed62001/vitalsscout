Frequently Asked Questions

        

          

            

              How is VitalsScout different from an accessibility overlay widget?
              
Overlay widgets inject surface scripts that often fail assistive technologies and regulatory enforcement (including EU EAA reviews). VitalsScout identifies and fixes underlying HTML semantics, form labels, contrast tokens, focus states, and target sizes directly in your codebase.

            

            

              Are there any monthly fees, subscriptions, or retainers?
              
No. VitalsScout operates exclusively as a one-time service business. You pay once per Website Diagnostic Audit ($20.86) or Website Audit + Fix ($48.86) with zero recurring billing or ongoing commitments.

            

            

              How do international payments work?
              
B2B clients receive an official one-time Payoneer Request a Payment invoice supporting US ACH bank debit, credit/debit cards, or local bank transfer (plus Fiverr checkout once linked). Work starts once payment is verified.

            

          

          

            

              What counts as one "unique template" in the 5-template scope?
              
One distinct page layout—for example: (1) Homepage, (2) Collection/Category grid, (3) Product or Pricing page, (4) Cart or Checkout Step 1, and (5) Contact or Blog post template. Auditing a template covers the reusable structure across those URLs.

            

            

              Does this include a legal VPAT or lawsuit immunity guarantee?
              
No. VitalsScout is an engineering service, not a law firm. We provide transparent technical audits and code fixes aligned with WCAG 2.2 AA and Core Web Vitals, not legal advice or formal legal certification.
