Privacy Notice

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

1. Static Storefront & Zero Tracking Cookies

This static storefront is hosted on GitHub Pages. VitalsScout does not install third-party ad pixels, behavioral analytics cookies, or cross-site trackers on this website. The interactive scope builder runs locally inside your browser and does not transmit data until you choose to verify a BNB Smart Chain transaction hash via public RPC nodes or submit your brief.

2. Client Confidentiality

Paid audit deliverables and repository/staging reviews are handled privately for the commissioning client and are never published in our public open-source repository. Only the fictional Roast & Craft Coffee demo pages and VitalsScout's own self-audit appear publicly as sample reports.

3. Payment Security

USDT (BEP20 on BNB Smart Chain) is the only accepted payment method. VitalsScout never collects, handles, or stores banking passwords, payment cards, wallet seed phrases, or private keys, and never initiates or signs cryptocurrency transactions on a customer's behalf.
