Privacy Notice

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

1. Static Storefront & Zero Tracking Cookies

This static storefront is hosted on GitHub Pages. VitalsScout does not install third-party ad pixels, behavioral analytics cookies, or cross-site trackers on this website. The interactive scope builder runs locally inside your browser and does not transmit data until you choose to submit a GitHub Issue or contact us directly.

Paid audit deliverables and repository/staging reviews are handled privately for the commissioning client and are never published in our public open-source repository. Only the fictional Roast & Craft Coffee demo pages and VitalsScout's own self-audit appear publicly as sample reports.

3. Payment Security

VitalsScout never collects or stores credit card numbers, CVVs, or banking passwords directly. All B2B payments are processed through official third-party payment platforms (Payoneer or Fiverr).
